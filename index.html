Case Study: CineMatch

In the era of streaming giants, content is abundant, but attention is scarce. Users suffer from "Analysis Paralysis"—too many choices and no idea what to watch.

The Solution:

CineMatch solves this discovery problem using Collaborative Filtering. It goes beyond simple "Top 10" lists by powering the famous "People who liked this also liked..." feature.

The Math:

By implementing a Matrix Factorization (SVD) algorithm, we mathematically represent taste using vectors and matrices, allowing the AI to predict exact future ratings for unseen movies.
